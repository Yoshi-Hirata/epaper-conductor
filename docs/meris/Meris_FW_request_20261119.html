R4. Let all colours finish togetherR4. 让所有颜色同时结束

Ask. Please build the official table so that every colour ends its drive at about the same tick. Or tell us if a level-0 hold at the very end is required.

请求。请制作正式的表，使每种颜色在大致相同的节拍结束驱动。或者请告诉我们，最后是否必须有电平 0 保持。

Why. With FW_260923 timing, the last drive pulse ends between tick 454 and tick 661, depending on the colour (see Appendix B). Each waveform has a different idle time at its end, so the colours finish at different moments on the panel.

原因。按 FW_260923 的时间，最后一个驱动脉冲在第 454 到第 661 节拍之间结束，因颜色而异（见附录 B）。每个波形末尾的空闲时间不同，所以各颜色在面板上结束的时刻不同。

Evidence. We moved each colour's trailing idle steps to directly after the shake (same steps, new order; for yellow, after its own longer shake). Result on the panel: all colours finish within 0.11 s, and the colours are unchanged. Measured on the panel, one tick takes about 11 ms, so the 662-tick sequence takes about 7.3 s.

证据。我们把每种颜色末尾的空闲步骤移到抖动之后（步骤相同，只改变顺序；黄色则移到它自己较长的抖动之后）。面板上的结果：所有颜色在 0.11 秒内结束，颜色没有变化。在面板上实测，一个节拍约为 11 ms，所以 662 节拍的序列约需 7.3 秒。

R5. Keep the built-in table authoritativeR5. 以内置颜色表为准

Ask. Please confirm our understanding below and keep the new behaviour.

请求。请确认我们下面的理解，并保持新的行为。

What we saw. One board showed only six colours repeating (codes 6-15 look like 0-5). This happened with the first 11/9 builds, with our test table on the FW_260923 program, and with plain MCB_16_0923.bin. With the re-sent 11/9 builds and the 11/18 build, the same board shows 16 colours.

我们看到的现象。有一块板子只显示重复的 6 种颜色（代码 6-15 看起来和 0-5 一样）。使用最初的 11/9 版、使用在 FW_260923 程序上的我们的测试表、以及使用原版 MCB_16_0923.bin 时都出现了这个现象。使用重新发来的 11/9 版和 11/18 版时，同一块板子显示 16 种颜色。

Our understanding. The FW_260923 program loads a waveform saved in the board's SPI flash in preference to the built-in table. The newer programs do not read it.

我们的理解。FW_260923 程序会优先加载保存在板子 SPI flash 中的波形，而不是内置颜色表。较新的程序不读取它。
