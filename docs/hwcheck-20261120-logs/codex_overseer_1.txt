## 4. Contradictions and suspected defects

- **#9 is allowed.** Restarting `epaper-ui` resets process bookkeeping while leaving Linux monotonic time running. G4 checks the packet’s estimated send time against current monotonic time, so a fresh packet can pass on the cached offset. The shared player still checks the show and burn. That makes reapplication reasonable in this case. The row does not verify a reboot, correct wall-clock restore, or exact position at recovery. After a real reboot, the docs’ “one poll” is insufficient as a guarantee when `_learn` needs two agreeing samples to replace a jumped offset. [Clock guard](C:/Users/sen2j/Claude/E-paper_H_WALL_BRICKS_Raspi/ui/beacon.py:228) · [Offset learning](C:/Users/sen2j/Claude/E-paper_H_WALL_BRICKS_Raspi/conductor/fleet.py:272)
- **#10 is by design.** ENDED stays ENDED under UDP STOP. `seen already` is a sequence-drop reason, so it does not establish why the first STOP copy did nothing. Sample `gen`, `applied`, and state together. Clear has its own path. A unit-local clear can follow natural end or an applied STOP when its show carries the flag. With Loop on, the compiled flag can be Conductor-only, so an unreachable unit may retain its slots pending HTTP recovery. [UDP STOP eligibility](C:/Users/sen2j/Claude/E-paper_H_WALL_BRICKS_Raspi/ui/beacon.py:150) · [Loop clear flags](C:/Users/sen2j/Claude/E-paper_H_WALL_BRICKS_Raspi/conductor/server.py:2534)
- **`127.0.0.1` is expected from the dummy links.** Route discovery scans all fleet links and normalizes loopback. Per-chunk transmission filters unicast by the entries it carries. `routes=3` therefore does not prove a loopback copy was sent. #12’s all-ten `skipped` count comes from iterating all configured links during adoption. The wording is misleading for links that never supplied status. The similar off-control value makes a beacon-specific defect less likely, but the trigger’s purpose and timing relative to music remain unverified. Capture preset state, scheduled trigger time, actual trigger time and T0 on a common clock, and watch the panel. The later `+1..2 ms` values measure local trigger scheduling, not PC-to-panel sync. Also, STOP arms the Conductor’s clear timer after the HTTP fan-out returns, so a concurrent START could cancel an unarmed timer and then have the old STOP arm it. The second sequence is a code-level race candidate, unverified on hardware.
